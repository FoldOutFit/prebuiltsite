The salon

A cozy grooming salon with a lot of heart.

Since opening in 2023, Devon and her team have built a loyal following in Delaware County. Reviewers describe a clean, adorable salon, gentle handling for nervous pups, photo updates during the visit, and full grooms that usually take about an hour to an hour and a half.

Services

Grooming for every pup

These are the services customers mention in public reviews. Call or text to ask about pricing for your dog.

Full grooms

Bath, haircut, and finish for all breeds and sizes, from Shih-Poos to Saint Bernards.

Bath & trim

A wash and tidy-up when your dog just needs a refresh.

De-matting

Nail trims

Nail care, even for pups other groomers have had trouble with.
